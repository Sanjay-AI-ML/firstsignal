## Four requested product features

- **Unread messages:** directory counts and explicit “Mark read through newest shown” are backed by participant-scoped read positions. A stale acknowledgment cannot regress a newer position; later-arriving messages remain unread. No public counterpart read receipt is introduced.
- **Idea revision history:** owner-only snapshots, field comparisons and confirmed content restoration are available for the selected idea. Recent snapshots are capped at 50. Public users and the ordinary owner moderation portal do not gain access to private history.
- **Saved searches:** users can save, apply and remove up to 20 private searches with the active filters. New matching-email alerts are unavailable; the API refuses enabling them and the frontend does not offer an activation control.
- **Email operations dashboard:** the owner-only Outbox has global metrics, all delivery states, deterministic 25-record pages and fixed diagnostic labels. Recipient addresses and private payloads are excluded. Unsafe manual retries that reset attempts were removed; automatic retries retain consent, recipient, frozen payload and safety windows. Configured settings do not establish live delivery.

## Important corrections to the supplied changes

Operation receipts must be atomic with their resource, not saved afterward or populated with a complete private workspace response. Profile saves/restores need current expected versions; restoration is content-only and must not republish a profile. Conversation acknowledgments must point to a real displayed message in the authorized conversation, not an arbitrary clock time. Closure is a manually reviewed pause, not scheduled deletion. Saved-search emails must not be presented as active. Delivery metrics must not call a delivered fifth attempt a failure, omit retry/sending backlog or expose raw provider errors. Monitoring failure must not become a healthy zero. Each correction is reflected in current source and the related isolated checks.

## Manual account closure procedure

This is an operator runbook for a future real request, not an automated portal feature and not a claim that any account was closed during this work.

1. **Verify the request and account.** Use the authenticated member request in `account_closures`, its exact namespaced Clerk owner ID and the existing Clerk instance. Confirm the request is still active. Do not select an account solely by matching a name, email or supplied profile ID. Keep the request reason and identity material out of public issues, logs and chat.
2. **Confirm the pause.** Check counts and exact ownership of that owner's profiles, pending outgoing introductions, notification preference and closure row. Confirm all their profiles are unlisted, outgoing pending introductions withdrawn and email preference off. A provider submission already in progress may still arrive. Do not clear member blocks, reports or another participant's history as an accidental side effect.
3. **Record a review decision.** Establish the applicable retention/support policy and any justified hold before promising erasure. There is no automatic retention date in this implementation. The ordinary owner portal does not list closure requests; a restricted operational review of the stored request is necessary until that workflow exists.
4. **Prepare the concrete plan.** Inventory counts and relationships for profiles, snapshots, tests, progress, saves, searches, messages, proposals, reports, blocks, notifications, outbox/receipts and read positions. Distinguish owner-private records from shared conversation and safety records. Specify deletion, anonymization, retained records and authentication changes with exact owner predicates. Avoid blind cascading deletes or whole-account email-based reassignment.
5. **Rehearse recovery.** Verify a current backup and restore it in an isolated environment. Rehearse the reviewed plan there, compare before/after counts and counterpart access, and confirm no unrelated data changes. Have an accountable operator approve the exact execution plan before irreversible operations; this runbook performs none.
6. **Complete authentication handling explicitly.** When the reviewed closure decision calls for it, revoke the exact account's Clerk sessions and complete the intended identity action through the authenticated provider controls. The application currently performs neither automatically. Verify the data and authentication outcomes separately; never claim a D1 pause deletes a Clerk account.
7. **Verify and communicate.** Recheck owner-scoped/public access, counterpart records, email suppression and retained safety/audit records. Record a minimal non-sensitive receipt and communicate what was actually completed and retained through the agreed private support channel. Do not promise erasure from backups or every external provider without checking their actual process. If the user cancels before completion, cancellation keeps profiles private and email off until deliberately changed.
